Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Study Buddy is a private homework helper for adults (18+) run by its owner on their own computers.

What we process

  • Your email address, used to sign you in (via Cloudflare Access one-time codes) and to remember that you confirmed you are 18+.
  • What you send: questions, photos and files. They are processed by AI models running on the owner's own computers (local Ollama). They are not sent to Google, OpenAI or any other cloud AI service.
  • Basic logs (time, which model answered, errors), kept for troubleshooting.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no selling or sharing your data, no third-party AI training.
  • Questions and uploads aren't stored in a database after the answer is returned. Your browser may keep your chat on your device.

Services involved

Cloudflare handles the secure connection and sign-in. Google Fonts may load the fonts.

Your choices

Ask the owner to remove your email and access at any time.
